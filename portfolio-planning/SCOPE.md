# Muse — Scope

> **Naming note:** "Muse" is the internal development codename only. It
> conflicts with existing products (see [Naming risk](#naming-risk) below) and
> will be renamed before public launch or any trademark filing.

## One-line description

A daily companion that writes to you in the voice of what you already love —
personalized to your favorite books, films, and poetry, with an optional tone
of voice and an optional nod to your cycle — instead of the generic
affirmations every other wellness app sends.

## Who it's for

Readers and story-minded people (initial persona: women, roughly 20–38, active
on Goodreads/StoryGraph/Bookstagram/BookTok or already journaling) who already
pay for at least one wellness or self-improvement subscription and want
content that reflects their actual taste, not a generic canon.

Explicitly **not** targeting: general "anyone stressed," enterprise/B2B
wellness, or a mass-market "everyone" audience in v1.

## Core v1 loop

1. **Onboarding** — name, favorite books/movies/poems (mapped to inferred
   themes, not stored as content to quote), default tone preference, optional
   cycle opt-in.
2. **Daily message generation**, combining:
   - Voice archetype (Gentle Poet, Stoic Guide, Warm Storyteller, ...)
   - Tone selector: **funny / motivating / deep** (user-selected per day or as
     a saved default)
   - Taste-derived themes (from favorite books/movies/poetry — used as mood
     and imagery inspiration, not as source text to imitate line-for-line)
   - Optional cycle phase (opt-in only, tone-shaping input, never diagnostic)
   - Optional citation pulled from a vetted **Reference Library** (see below)
3. **Save/favorite** any message into a personal **Commonplace Book**.
4. **Light reflection reply** — a one-line "how did that land?" prompt after
   each message, to deepen personalization and investment.

## The Reference Library (citation safety mechanism)

Citations are never freely generated by the LLM. They come from a curated,
tagged table of quotes split by legal status:

- **Public domain** (pre-1929 US works, classical poetry, mythology, folklore)
  → safe to quote in full, verbatim.
- **Modern/copyrighted works** → never quoted verbatim. The system references
  the *theme or archetype* the user's favorite work represents, not the
  author's actual sentences.

This avoids both the copyright exposure and the hallucination/misattribution
risk of letting an LLM "remember" and recite quotes on demand.

## Near-term extensions (v1.5)

- **Recommendation coda** — an optional closing line suggesting a next
  book/film, grounded against a real metadata API (Google Books / Open
  Library / TMDB) so the LLM never invents a nonexistent title. Framed as an
  emotional aside, not a discovery/browse feature. Can carry affiliate links
  (Bookshop.org, Amazon Associates, streaming affiliate programs) as a
  secondary revenue stream.
- **Rituals** — 30–60 second morning/evening/grounding/reset micro-practices.
- **Audio narration** via ElevenLabs, private per-user storage in the `tts`
  bucket.
- **Annual/seasonal recap** ("Muse Wrapped") — favorite themes, saved
  entries, shareable card, timed reactivation moment.

## Later roadmap

- **Printed keepsake** — an optional printed/bound "Year in Words" book
  compiled from a user's Commonplace Book (StoryWorth-style one-time-purchase
  product; proven model for converting accumulated daily content into
  something people pay real money for).
- **Serialized narrative arcs ("Muse Stories")** — human-authored story
  skeletons that the AI personalizes and paces, not invents from scratch. This
  is the long-term differentiation moat (see [MARKETING.md](MARKETING.md)):
  continuity and authored IP are much harder to clone than a prompt.
- **Companion chat** — structured, non-therapeutic reflective conversation
  with mandatory crisis-resource redirect logic.
- **Creator marketplace** — external writers/voice artists contribute arcs,
  rituals, and voices; revenue share.
- **Mobile app** (Expo), after the web habit loop is validated.

## What Muse explicitly is NOT

- **Not** a cycle-tracking/fertility utility — that is Flo/Clue territory
  (huge scale, clinical trust, data-first). Cycle input here is a
  tone-personalization signal only, never a health insight, prediction, or
  diagnostic feature.
- **Not** a book/movie discovery or social network — that is
  Goodreads/StoryGraph/Letterboxd territory. Recommendations are a small,
  emotionally-framed coda, never a browse/search/social feature.
- **Not** a therapy or counseling product. Companion chat (when built) is
  structured reflection only, with explicit non-therapy disclaimers.
- **Not** a general-purpose AI wellness companion competing head-on with
  Calm/Headspace/Woebot-style products on breadth.

## Legal and safety boundaries (binding constraints)

- **Cycle data:** opt-in only, manually entered (never inferred from other
  signals), no third-party analytics/ad SDKs anywhere near that data path,
  one-tap deletion, plain-language disclosure. (Directly informed by the FTC's
  2021 enforcement action against Flo Health for sharing menstrual-cycle data
  with third-party analytics/ad platforms — the category's leading cautionary
  precedent.)
- **Citations:** verbatim quoting only from the vetted, public-domain
  Reference Library; copyrighted works referenced by theme only.
- **Companion chat (when built):** explicit "not therapy" disclaimers,
  mandatory crisis-resource redirect for self-harm language, consider an 18+
  gate given active 2025 state-level restrictions on AI systems presenting as
  mental health support.
- **Voice archetypes:** original characters only — no impersonation of real,
  identifiable people (right-of-publicity risk); follow ElevenLabs' usage
  policy; disclose AI-generated voice per emerging transparency norms.
- **Subscriptions:** one-tap cancellation, compliant with "click-to-cancel"
  norms (FTC and various state auto-renewal rules).
- **Recommendations:** only ever surface real, API-verified titles — never an
  LLM-invented book/film.

## Naming risk

Live checks against currently-operating products found direct conflicts with
the "Muse" name specifically:

- `choosemuse.com` — InteraXon's established meditation/sleep EEG wellness
  brand.
- `muse.ai` — an existing AI companion/assistant product using this exact
  name.
- Additional prior use: "Musely," Adobe Muse (legacy), the band Muse's
  merchandising trademarks.

**Action:** run a proper trademark clearance search and rename before any
public launch, App Store listing, or trademark filing. Continue using "Muse"
as the internal/dev codename only (see [PLANNING.md](PLANNING.md)).
